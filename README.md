## 📚 Overview

This repository contains computational modeling data, scripts, and supplementary materials for the research paper:

**"In silico modeling and bioquided isolation synergize to discover antifungal compounds from bitter orange peel"**  
*Bendaha et al., In Silico Research in Biomedicine, 2026 (DOI: 10.1016/j.insi.2026.100419)*

The study demonstrates an integrated computational and experimental approach to discover antifungal compounds from *Citrus aurantium* (bitter orange) peel, a sustainable agricultural byproduct.

## 🎯 Key Findings

- **Bioactive fractions** identified from hexane (SPH) and acetone (SPA) extracts
- **Major compounds**: Limonene (36.15%) and Naringenin (23.94%)
- **Mechanism**: Terpenoids bind to ergosterol in fungal membranes (ΔG_bind: -6.07 to -6.40 kcal/mol)
- **Drug-like properties**: Caffeic acid, catechin, and p-coumaric acid show favorable pharmacokinetic profiles
